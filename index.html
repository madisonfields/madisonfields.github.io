How I work

The same loop on every account, whether it’s a startup or an enterprise site in ten languages.

  1. 01

    Learn how people ask

    Keyword, prompt and SERP research, plus the questions sales and support hear. This is the anthropologist part.

  2. 02

    Fix the foundation

    Crawlability, indexation, redirects, page speed, schema and llms.txt, so search engines and LLMs can read the site.

  3. 03

    Build answer-ready content

    Pillars, glossaries, answer-first intros and FAQs that AI tools can quote and people want to click.

  4. 04

    Track prompts, not just rankings

    AI Overview and ChatGPT citations, share of voice against competitors, and the prompts buyers actually use.

  5. 05

    Tie it to pipeline

    Key events, CRO and A/B tests, and quarterly reviews that show leadership what search is worth.
